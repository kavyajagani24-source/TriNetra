## 2. Anticipated Judge Questions & Defensible Answers

### Q1: "Why do you have false positives on crowded traffic videos?"
> **Answer**: "In 2D camera footage of dense urban intersections, vehicle bounding boxes naturally overlap in perspective without physical contact. Rather than hiding this, our system introduces a **3-Tier Operational Triage Policy**:
> - **Tier 1 ($\text{Score} \ge 0.865$)**: Dispatches emergency services immediately. On our 16-video held-out test split, Tier 1 achieved **100% dispatch precision with zero false alarms**.
> - **Tier 2 ($0.750 \le \text{Score} < 0.865$)**: Flags near-misses and perspective overlaps as **OPERATOR REVIEW REQUIRED**, preventing false emergency dispatches.
> - **Tier 3 ($\text{Score} < 0.750$)**: Clears normal traffic with **zero missed collisions** (100% collision recall across all 8 positive test accidents)."

### Q2: "Did you tune your decision threshold on the test set?"
> **Answer**: "No. To maintain scientific integrity, the operating threshold $T^* = 0.820$ was selected **strictly on the 14 validation videos**, maximizing validation F1 and balanced accuracy. When applied without modification to the 16 untouched test videos, it achieved **75.0% accuracy, 66.7% precision, 100.0% recall, and an F1 score of 0.800**."

### Q3: "Did you train your own ANPR model on Indian roads?"
> **Answer**: "No, and claiming so would be scientifically inaccurate because the Person 4 dataset has zero license plate ground truth annotations. We integrated a pretrained YOLO11n plate detector with EasyOCR and engineered a **5-frame track-level temporal majority voting engine** with Indian license plate regex validation. When plates are blurry or unreadable, the system honestly reports `UNREADABLE` rather than hallucinating text."

### Q4: "What happens if a vehicle collides and the driver stays at the scene to help?"
> **Answer**: "Our 5-state Hit-and-Run FSM handles this explicitly. In video `v4.mov`, the collision occurs at 7.23s between vehicles #8 and #10. Both vehicles remain stationary post-impact. The system outputs `COLLISION_WITHOUT_DEPARTURE` and sets `is_hit_and_run_candidate: false`. Hit-and-run is only flagged when one vehicle flees at high velocity and exits the camera field of view while the other remains stationary."

### Q5: "Why don't you use ByteTrack?"
> **Answer**: "We audited our tracker implementation and verified it is `UrbianTracker`, a custom hybrid IoU and normalized centroid distance matching algorithm. ByteTrack relies on Kalman filters and low-confidence association cascades. Our tracker is tailored for urban traffic intersections with sudden stops without Kalman overshoot."

### Q6: "Why is ROC-AUC / PR-AUC reported as 1.000 if your test set still has 4 false positives?"
> **Answer**: "ROC-AUC and PR-AUC measure **ranking quality** across all possible thresholds, whereas the confusion matrix reflects a single operational threshold locked on the validation set ($T^* = 0.820$). The interaction model perfectly ranked positive collisions above normal traffic, but the validation-selected threshold still admitted 4 borderline false positives in dense congestion. This is why we deploy a **3-Tier Operational Triage Policy** rather than relying on a single binary cutoff."

### Q7: "Your neural model confidence is only 50.7%, why is your incident score 83.2%?"
> **Answer**: "They represent distinct evidence sources. The 50.7% is the standalone visual sequence model's raw output. The 83.2% is the fused incident evidence score, which incorporates the pairwise vehicle interaction model (88.0%), kinematic evidence, and localized optical flow. We deliberately report all three values separately in the incident JSON and telemetry for transparency and forensic auditing."

### Q8: "What is the final defensible statement of Person 4?"
> **Answer**:
> *'On our 16-video held-out test set, the pairwise interaction engine achieved 100% collision recall, 66.7% precision, and 0.80 F1 at an operating threshold selected exclusively on the validation set, with Tier 1 emergency dispatch achieving 100% precision and zero false alarms.'*
